# Hani Roustom — Research / Content Brief

## Identity

- **Name:** Hani Roustom
- **Current role:** CEO, Friday Harbour Resort, Innisfil, Ontario, Canada.

Hani Roustom is a Canadian hospitality executive with more than 25 years of experience in luxury hotels, resorts, destination development, hospitality operations, and community building. His career has included senior leadership positions with Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts, Shangri-La Hotels and Resorts, The Hazelton Hotel, and Friday Harbour Resort.

His professional positioning is strongly connected to:

- Luxury hospitality
- Destination development
- Guest experience
- Service excellence
- Asset valuation and profitability
- Community building
- Hospitality leadership
- Mentorship and education
- Fashion, culture and entertainment
- Toronto's hospitality and tourism ecosystem
- International travel and global perspective

His LinkedIn profile currently describes his philosophy around hospitality as being about more than hotels and resorts — specifically about creating destinations.

## Current Position — Friday Harbour Resort

Hani became CEO of Friday Harbour Resort in **February 2021**.

Friday Harbour is a large luxury resort community on Lake Simcoe in Innisfil, Ontario. The destination includes:

- An 18-hole golf course
- Canada's largest inland marina
- A 200-acre nature sanctuary
- Beach Club
- Tennis
- Outdoor pools
- Fitness facilities
- Retail
- Restaurants
- Residential properties
- Hospitality and resort experiences

The official Friday Harbour material describes Hani as a thought leader, community builder and global citizen, as well as an expert in luxury hospitality and asset valuation.

His stated focus at Friday Harbour is not solely the guest experience. The official profile specifically emphasizes his interest in creating a positive impact on the surrounding community.

The resort's stated vision is to establish Friday Harbour as an exceptional all-seasons waterside resort, with its mission centered around nature, luxury and service.

## Career Background

### Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts

- Joined Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts in **2006** at the Washington, D.C. property as Director of Restaurants and Bars.
- Transferred to Toronto in **2008**.
- Involved in the development/opening of the new flagship Four Seasons Hotel Toronto on Yorkville Avenue.
- Led the opening of **Café Boulud**, working with renowned chef Daniel Boulud.
- The Four Seasons Toronto subsequently became the first hotel in Canada to hold both **Forbes Five-Star** and **CAA Five Diamond** recognition (per the Hazelton announcement documenting Roustom's career).

### Shangri-La Hotel Toronto

- Joined in **2013** as part of the executive team, serving as **Executive Assistant Manager, Food & Beverage**.
- The Park Group's profile states that from 2013–2016 he was part of the team that established the relatively new Shangri-La Toronto brand and helped the hotel achieve **CAA Five Diamond** recognition.

### The Hazelton Hotel

- Became **General Manager** of The Hazelton Hotel in Toronto in **2016**.
- The Hazelton is a luxury boutique hotel in Toronto's Yorkville neighbourhood.
- His work there became one of the defining parts of his career.
- When Hani joined, the hotel was ranked around **No. 23** among Toronto hotels on TripAdvisor. Within approximately six months it reached **No. 1**, and remained there for **four consecutive years**.
- The Park Group describes his work at The Hazelton as a repositioning and turnaround strategy that resulted in:
  - Increased occupancy
  - Increased profitability
  - Increased asset valuation
  - Strengthening of the property's luxury positioning

This demonstrates that Hani's career is not simply operational hotel management: it also includes commercial performance, repositioning, brand development and asset value creation.

### Be Our Guest

- Hani **founded Be Our Guest**, a hospitality-industry high-school co-op program.
- Launched to introduce high-school students to the hospitality and tourism industry and develop the next generation of hospitality professionals.
- Provides students with exposure to: hospitality employers, mentorship, workplace placements, industry field trips, hospitality careers.
